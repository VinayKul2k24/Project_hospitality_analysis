# 🏨 Project: Hospitality Analysis – ATILQ Hotel Group

This project focuses on data analysis for **ATILQ**, a chain of hospitality properties. It includes in-depth exploration of financial, operational, and occupancy-related metrics across properties and time to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📊 Objective

To extract insights from historical data to:

* Monitor trends in expenses and revenue
* Understand occupancy and seasonality patterns
* Compare performance across different ATILQ properties
* Identify high-cost categories and operational inefficiencies
* Support strategic planning and forecasting

---

## 🧱 Tech Stack

* **Python 3.9+**
* **Pandas**, **NumPy** – Data manipulation
* **Matplotlib**, **Seaborn**, **Plotly** – Visualizations
* **Jupyter Notebooks** – Exploratory analysis

---

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

### ✅ 1. Expense Analysis

* Total and average spend per category
* High-cost departments (e.g., housekeeping, food services)
* Year-over-year (YoY) changes

### ✅ 2. Occupancy & Seasonality

* Monthly occupancy rates
* Peak vs off-season trends
* Property-level occupancy comparison

### ✅ 3. Revenue vs Profitability

* Gross revenue trends
* Profit margins by month/property
* Forecasting future performance (basic models)

---

## 🧪 Sample Outputs

* 📊 Expense distribution pie charts
* 📉 Occupancy line plots by property
* 📌 Heatmaps of category spending
* 📆 Time series of revenue trends

All visualizations are designed to support internal reporting and executive decision-making.
